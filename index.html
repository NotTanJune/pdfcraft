PdfCraft

A personal build of PdfCraft, the open-source PDF reader and editor, for macOS.

This fork adds a few things on top of the upstream app:

  • Open and save PDFs in your own Google Drive or OneDrive.
  • A searchable document library, and an encrypted vault.
  • Opening Word, Markdown, LaTeX, CSV and Excel files as PDFs, and exporting tables back out.
  • Link previews, and asking an AI model of your choice about selected text, with your own key.

Everything runs on your Mac. There is no PdfCraft server, account or analytics.
