Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

This policy covers the personal build of PdfCraft published at github.com/NotTanJune/pdfcraft, a desktop app that runs on your own Mac.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no server, no account system, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developer never receives your documents, your files, your sign-in tokens or anything about how you use the app.

Google Drive and OneDrive

When you choose to connect Google Drive or OneDrive, you sign in with Google or Microsoft in your own browser. The app then:

  • lists the folders and files in your drive, so you can pick one;
  • downloads a file you choose to open, onto your Mac;
  • uploads a file only when you choose Save to drive or Save a copy to drive;
  • reads your account's email address, only to show which account is connected.

The app talks directly to Google's or Microsoft's servers from your Mac. Your sign-in token is stored in your Mac's keychain and is never sent anywhere else. Data from Google and Microsoft is used only to provide these features to you. It is not sold, shared, transferred to anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To disconnect, choose Disconnect in the app's Cloud drives window, which removes the stored token. You can also remove access at any time from your Google or Microsoft account's security settings.

AI and link previews

Both are off until you turn them on. Ask AI sends the text you select, and your question, to the AI service whose address and key you entered. Link previews, when enabled in Preferences, fetch the title of a web page you point at. Neither sends anything to the developer.

Your documents

Documents stay on your Mac, in your iCloud Drive if you use the library, or in the drive you chose. Vault documents are encrypted on your Mac with a passphrase only you know.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. For questions, use the support email address shown on the Google sign-in screen when you connect.
